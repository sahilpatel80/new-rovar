How We Build It

From Concept to Guest Room

Our end-to-end manufacturing process ensures every product that leaves our facility meets the highest standards of quality, safety, and consistency.

01
🧪

R&D & Formulation

Our in-house chemists develop and test formulations for every new product, balancing sensory experience, skin safety, stability, and cost-efficiency for B2B hospitality needs.

02
📦

Ingredient Sourcing

We procure premium active ingredients from verified, internationally-compliant suppliers. All raw materials undergo COA verification and incoming quality inspection before production begins.

03
⚗️

Batch Manufacturing

Controlled batch production follows GMP-compliant procedures in our clean manufacturing halls, with automated mixing, filling, and sealing lines for consistent output at scale.

04
🔍

Quality Control

Every batch is subjected to in-process QC checks including pH, viscosity, microbial load, and stability tests. Only fully compliant batches proceed to packaging and dispatch.

05
🎨

Custom Packaging

From bespoke label design to branded carton printing, our in-house packaging team handles all customization — supporting custom shapes, label artwork, and hotel-specific branding elements.

06
🚚

Packing & Dispatch

Finished goods are packed to hospitality-grade transport standards, with barcoding, lot traceability, and timely dispatch to hotel properties and distribution partners across India.

Inside Our Plant

A Facility Built for Precision

Our 20,000+ sq. ft. facility in Ahmedabad is designed around clean manufacturing, energy efficiency, and scalable B2B production. Every operational zone is purpose-built for the hotel amenities category.

  • 🏭

    GMP-Compliant Production Halls

    Segregated wet & dry zones with positive pressure airflow, temperature monitoring, and scheduled sanitation protocols.

  • 🔬

    In-House R&D Laboratory

    Equipped with microbiology, stability, and sensory testing capabilities — enabling rapid formulation and claim validation.

  • ⚡

    Automated Filling Lines

    High-speed liquid and cream filling lines with volumetric accuracy and zero-cross-contamination design.

  • 📋

    Full Batch Traceability

    Every batch is tracked from raw material intake through final dispatch using our lot-based documentation system.

Standards & Compliance

Built to International Standards

Every product we manufacture complies with Indian regulatory requirements and aligns with internationally recognized cosmetics and personal care industry standards.

🎍

GMP Certified

Good Manufacturing Practice compliance ensures every batch is produced under controlled, hygienic, and documented conditions.

🛡️

BIS & CDSCO Compliant

All cosmetics and personal care products comply with Indian Bureau of Standards and Central Drugs Standard Control regulations.

🌿

No Harmful Ingredients
